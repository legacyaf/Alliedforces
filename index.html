Server rules

The full, current rulebook lives in Discord. Joining our servers means you agree to these rules and our Terms of Service.

  1. Respect everyone. No hate speech, harassment, threats or discrimination — in game, in voice or in Discord.
  2. No cheating or exploiting. Hacks, macros, duping, glitching into bases and abusing bugs lead to a permanent ban. Report bugs in a ticket instead.
  3. PvP only where it's allowed. Outside PvP zones and raid windows, the server is PvE. Check the map and the current raid schedule.
  4. Raid within the rules. Raiding is only allowed during raid week, following the zone rules announced for that wipe.
  5. Build fairly. No blocking roads, traders, POIs or loot spawns, and no building in restricted areas.
  6. Vehicles. Use parking meters to protect your vehicles. Vehicle loss from server issues is handled through a ticket.
  7. Staff decisions. Disputes go through a Discord ticket, not public channels. Staff have the final say.

Monetization compliance

Allied Forces follows the Bohemia Interactive Server Monetization Rules for DayZ private shard servers. In short:

  • Donations are voluntary and give nothing in return.
  • Paid supporter packages are only sold once our server is approved and listed by Bohemia Interactive.
  • Nothing sold affects gameplay: no weapons, ammo, gear, vehicles, in-game currency, discounts, bases or spawns.
  • We do not sell content from mods without permission from the mod authors.
  • If Bohemia's rules change, these packages change with them.

Think something we offer breaks these rules? Tell us in a Discord ticket, or report it to Bohemia Interactive directly.

Privacy Policy

Last updated:

What we collect

  • Game data: your Steam ID, in-game name, connection logs and in-game activity, used to run the servers and enforce rules.
  • Discord data: your Discord username and ID, and messages or tickets you send us.
  • Website accounts: when you sign in with Discord, we receive your Discord ID, username, avatar and email from Discord. If you add them, we also store your Steam64 ID and in-game name. Accounts are stored with our database provider, Supabase.
  • Community content: posts, photos, comments and likes you make are public. Direct messages can only be read by you and the person you're talking to — AF staff can't read them unless one of you reports a message, which sends a copy of that message to staff.
  • Payment data: payments are handled by our payment provider. We receive your name, email, Steam/Discord ID and what you bought — never your full card details.

How we use it

To run the servers, deliver supporter perks, handle tickets, prevent cheating and comply with the law. We don't sell your data.

Who we share it with

Only with services we need to operate: our game server host, Discord, our payment provider, and anti-cheat or admin tools. We may share data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

As long as needed for the purposes above. Ban records may be kept indefinitely to enforce bans.

Your choices

You can ask us to see or delete your data by opening a Discord ticket or emailing . Some data (like ban and payment records) may need to be kept.
